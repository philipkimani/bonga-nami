# Family Planning

## What is family planning?

Family planning means choosing whether and when to have children, and how many. It lets you finish school, work, rest between pregnancies and plan your life. It is your choice. You can use family planning without anyone's permission.

Family planning services are free at public health facilities in Kenya.

## The main methods

| Method | How long it lasts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Implant** | 3 to 5 years, depending on the type | A small rod placed under the skin of the upper arm. Very effective. Can be removed any time, and fertility returns quickly. |
| **Copper IUD (coil)** | Up to 10 years or more | A small device placed in the womb by a health worker. Very effective. No hormones. Can be removed any time. |
| **Injection** | 3 months | One injection every three months. Fertility can take a few months longer to return after stopping. |
| **Daily pill** | While you take it | One pill every day, at about the same time. |
| **Male and female condoms** | Each time you have sex | The only methods that also protect against HIV and other sexually transmitted infections. |
| **Breastfeeding method** | Up to 6 months after birth | Only works if the baby is under six months, you give only breast milk day and night, and your periods have not returned. |

The implant and the IUD are the most effective. They work without you having to remember anything.

## Side effects

Changes to your period are the most common side effect of hormonal methods. Periods may become lighter, heavier, irregular, or stop for a while. This is not dangerous and does not harm your fertility. If side effects bother you, go back to the clinic — you can switch to another method.

## Emergency contraception

If you had sex without protection, or a condom broke, emergency contraception can prevent pregnancy.

- **Emergency pills** work best the sooner you take them. Take them as soon as possible, and within 3 days (72 hours). They are available from health facilities and chemists.
- **A copper IUD** put in by a health worker within 5 days is the most effective emergency method, and it then keeps working as regular family planning.

Emergency pills are not an abortion. They do not harm an existing pregnancy. They are for emergencies, not regular use.

## Myths about family planning

**"Family planning makes you unable to have children later."**
False. Fertility returns after stopping any method. After the injection it can take a few months longer, but it does return.

**"The implant can move around the body."**
False. It stays where it was placed in the arm.

**"You must have had a baby before you can use family planning."**
False. Women who have never been pregnant can use any method, including the implant and the IUD.

**"Condoms are only for people who do not trust each other."**
False. Condoms are a sensible way to protect both partners from pregnancy and infections.

## Spacing pregnancies

After a birth, waiting at least two years before trying for the next pregnancy is healthiest for you and your next baby. You can start most methods soon after giving birth — ask at the clinic before you leave.
